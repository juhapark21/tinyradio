## Why 
I made it because I like tiny things (my most prized pencilcase posession is a tiny calculator), I like keychains, and I like radios. I'm also new to hardware stuff so it was nice to get started with a simpler receiver. That, and I had impulse-bought RDA5807 chips a year or so ago.
I also wanted a neat trinket I could give to ham radio people when I meet them at conventions and stuff. 
Additionally, my current radio is rather large... so I can't really carry it around anywhere. 

## Process 
The development process is logged in detail [here](https://stasis.hackclub.com/dashboard/projects/cmndil887006o01qo4aqrqvzn). It took me quite a while (~1 month of consistent work) because I was pretty much new to PCB design. I learned a lot about KiCad and the design process through this project.

## Next steps 
I want to try making multiple at a time (larger scale) so that I have enough to go around. Also, harder projects (tiny calculator...).
